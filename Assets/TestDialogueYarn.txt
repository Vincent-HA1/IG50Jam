---

<<show_cutscene DialogueManager 0>>
The bathroom, cramped. 

The overhead light flickered intermittently, casting a sickly yellow glow across cracked white tiles. Ashe stands before the porcelain sink, shoulders slumped, hands resting against the edges with a toothbrush between her fingers as the sound of running water fills the air.

Her gaze is fixed on her reflection in the mirror, though there is little focus in her eyes. Dark circles shadow them, and her expression is drawn with exhaustion, her lips resting in a faint, unhappy line.

She has been standing there for some time, too tired to move and too lost in thought to look away. Her mind is pushing unwanted memories into her skull about her father.

Teddy. A Police Officer. A professional rule-follower, an occasional terrible-joke enthusiast, and the sort of man who thought a uniform could fix just about anything. He used to run towards danger while everyone else ran away.

Then came the accident.

One moment, he was a police officer doing his job. The next, he was in a hospital bed, one leg gone, and neither of them had the faintest idea what came next.

A reminder of a disinfectant smell.

Relentless hospital beeping.

Teddy staring at the space where his leg used to be.

The urge to say something comforting, but Ashe's emotional support software had crashed. So she stood there, silent and frozen in time.

The doctors told Ashe how lucky she was that he survived.

Ashe: Lucky.
That's what everyone called it. As if Teddy losing a leg was an acceptable trade-off for staying alive.
Ashe: People love saying the right things when they don't have to live with the aftermath.
A forced, crooked smile.
A heavy sigh.
<<play_sfx_one_shot SoundManager wash_face>>
A splash of cold water over her face. 
<<stop_sfx SoundManager>>
The hope that it'll wash away the thoughts along with the toothpaste.
<<play_dialogue_delay DialogueManager Ashe_1>>
